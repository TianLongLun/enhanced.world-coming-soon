The gap

Nobody builds for the network being gone,
supplies disrupted, for natural crisis or war
- it's unprofitable situations.

Yet, technology allows it.

Everything you use assumes the infrastructure holds. Communication, Medical Help, Services, access to Food and Water, Navigation. Challenging that assumption isn't profitable — we have to stop waiting. One day You or your relatives can be in need of it - let's build the life saving system.

Blackouts, backbone failures, solar events, supply-chain breaks, war. Each strips a different layer from the stack societies run on: connection, exchange, communication, payment. When it happens, there is currently nothing free, tested, and ready to deploy at scale.

ECS is being built as infrastructure of last resort — a custom set system with scenario-specific presets, designed to work when nothing else does. It runs on the assumption that the people who need it will not be technical, will not have power, and will not have time to read a manual.

The architecture is ready, plans for various depth of details are ready for experienced peers validation and tweaks.

It's holistic take on what I would need / like to have in 8 variants of scenarios - ready and guiding-by-hand on real life use. We need developers of various depth of insight, but also wide range of specialists and skilled experts on survival and non-digital helpful in crisis skills, and individuals willing to help with more mundane tasks.

It is open source because a system whose survival depends on one company, one server, or one person is not resilient. It is pro bono because the people who will need it cannot pay. It's modular to fit for different possibilities and as multiplication of possibilities is another principle of safety.

Technology is open, waiting there. Blocks and architecture is there, and storm is coming. We need to make it real, the open emergency ark.

Four scenarios

Not one generic "offline mode."

Each scenario assumes a different kind of threat volume, technology and direness. Part of the project is scenarios repleted technical specification mapping, circumstances, user available tech, power and in-field time of usage.
A design that survives a blackout does not survive automated signal-hunting. So there are four, each with "light" and "hard" variants.

A

Blackout / Dead-Internet

ISP or backbone failure, regional outage. The network and most of communication is down; the hardware isn't. System and services somehow operates, no cashless payments. Nobody is hunting you, in hard version criminal activity high.

Threat Low · Priority High
B

Civilization Collapse

Long-term systems, supply and manufacturing failure. Proxy comms, barter, resource coordination. Scavenged hardware mostly.
Encountered people can be helpful saviors, neutral, an active treat, or spreading illness.

Threat Medium · Priority Mid
C

Total War

Automated signal scanning, active node hunting. Emitting is dangerous. Metadata is a target list. Impersonation / decoy possibility.

Grave threat level / complete spectrum monitoring. Long range avoidance, disruptions, stealth.

Threat Highest · Priority Mid
D

Hybrid War

Extreme hackers threat, compromised infrastructure, surveillance state, economy and net is working more or less.

War mobility restrictions, emitting can be dangerous - various threat and seriousness levels, GPS and services can be temporary unavailable.

Threat High · Priority Low

Hackers, Natural Crisis, Small Solar Flare, Large Malfunction. The short to mid term disruption of avibility.

Carrington Event-like Solar Flare, Trade and Logistics Disruptions in Global Economy, Fallout / World War 3

AI becked invasion, Military operations against civilians, Extinction Threat All-in War / Homicide

Anything the first three don't cover. Weaponizeation of all-systems - not only digital.

Architecture

Every layer encloses the next.

Swap a plugin without touching the core. Swap a module without touching the framework. Nothing depends on one component, one build, or one person. Distributions consists of set-modules and verified plugins, but any combination / customization can be made.

L1 — Outer
Build Package
Scenario-specific apps type composition: Client · NF relay node · PON pocket point · HQ
L2
Core Framework
Routing, security, storage, device I/O, process management. Audited and frozen.
L3
Modules
Communication · maps · community boards · reputation · data exchange · Extra functionalities
L4 — Core
Plugins
Encryption, compression, transmission, verification — swapped at runtime via the inject-matrix.

Trust escalates the same way access does

You start by reading. You end — if the work earns it — inside the Black Box, where encryption and safety-critical modules live under gated review, in a repository separate from the public one.

Who's needed

Claim a problem. Not a job.

Open an issue for the piece you want to own. No résumé, no interview, no gatekeeper. Every contributor is credited permanently, and earns a share if their module ever generates revenue.

Cryptographers & security engineers

Offline-first encryption with no real-time validation. Node authentication without a certificate authority. Post-quantum key exchange for a network that may need to outlive the state that attacked it.

Distributed systems & P2P engineers

Gossip protocols over links measured in bytes per minute. Byzantine fault tolerance where nodes lie on purpose. Consensus across clusters that may never reconnect.

Hardware & embedded specialists

Turning dead phones, routers, and salvaged components into relay nodes. Power harvesting. Builds that survive conditions the datasheet doesn't cover.

Systems architects

What does Scenario C actually cost in watts, latency, and emission risk? How does the network behave at 10% of nodes? At 1%? Graceful degradation is the whole discipline here.

Documentation & localisation

Instructions a frightened, non-technical person can follow with no power and no signal. In their language. An error here costs more than a bug does.

Governance, legal & organisers

Licensing that resists corporate capture. Contributor rights that survive the project outgrowing anyone. Onboarding a distributed volunteer base that has never met.

Craftsmen, Specialist & Experts

Historical and real-world skills that can be helpful in any of scenarios - survival, crafts, manual skills, analog coding / communication, herbs, agriculture, medicine all that can be useful, guidelines and hints for real life, no expertise users.

Organizations, Ambassadors & all willing to help

This project is huge and we need all helps possible - don't be shy! Let's do it! As it's made for public benefit all kind of organizations are welcomed to help and use our software!

Governance

The moment one person is essential, resilience has already failed.

This project is deliberately built without founder authority. That is not modesty — it is the same design principle as the mesh itself.

Who started it?
Karol, a systems architect who got tired of waiting for someone else to build resilience infrastructure. He drew the blueprints and wrote the first documentation. That's the designed extent of it.
Is he in charge?
No. No veto, not even author's privilege. If the architecture is wrong, say so publicly — and it changes, or you fork it and build the better one. Only in case when the community is unable to conclude the decision, or in case of idea of shuting down the project, Karol retains decisive voice.
How are decisions made?
Proposal → 72-hour minimum discussion → vote → logged → implemented by volunteers. The exact mechanism is still open, and shaping it is itself an unclaimed problem. I'm planing to leave all governance to the community - under the banner of to be established non-profit foundation for legal representation (for consideration).
What do contributors get?
Permanent credit on GitHub. Governance weight proportional to work done. Free use of the system in your own country. A revenue share if a module you built is ever licensed commercially.
What's the licence?
AGPLv3 for open and non-commercial use, globally. Commercial deployment requires a written agreement — specifically so that a corporation cannot enclose what a community built. Terms in 05_LICENSE.md.
And the AI policy?
Online AI must not be used on whole modules, files, or repositories. Locally run models only for Core Framework and Black Box work. Fragment-level help is fine.
