A 7-day window, 3 +1 round , real-infrastructure adversarial cybersecurity exercise. PRO enterprise teams and EDU university teams compete on standalone, dedicated server environments under sustained adversarial pressure simultaneously generating academic-grade behavioral and technical telemetry that does not exist in any public dataset. Benchmarking different protocols, validating human - tools (UI, UX, AI) and human - human efficiency. It's arena ground for complete SOC to complete, integrating very advanced real life CTF (various type of flags). Metrics, Benchmark and Compete Scoring. Low points for DDoS and high for stealth, creativity, resistance. Team: Red + Blue + Setup | Response, Detection, Recovery / Stealth, Modification, Time. Any approach AI / Bot / Human. Rules are slightly different for EDU and PRO.

Data Size are estimates and depends on exact participants and used tools, inside documentation might vary - strict est. after test #2.
