B Data Gathered — Types, Formats & Volume TECHNICAL & BEHAVIORAL TELEMETRY
#gathered +

Technical and behavioral telemetry is gathered, stored and backed-up offline, with appropriate anonymity tiers applied before further processing. Non-anonymised data is never exposed or used externally. Custom data gathering will not be excluded from sets and will never occur in stealth.

Each set can be subdivided in separate: technical, behavioral telemetry and extras - in form of complete archive ("Forensic" or "RAW") or selective and extensively processed ("ML-Ready")and can be acquired in accordance to release policy. Local Buffer Redundancy.

ML-Ready — Definition

ML-ready datasets contain the following processed layers, ready for direct ingestion into ML pipelines without additional pre-processing:

  • NetFlow metadata in place of full PCAP (except during incident windows)
  • Incident-triggered PCAP only — estimated ~20–30% of R3 duration
  • Host logs as compressed labeled JSON (Sysmon/auditd → ~40% of raw size)
  • Behavioral video, H.265 activity-adaptive compression (~3–5× vs raw; video constitutes ~50% of total ML-ready volume)
  • Full L1+L2+L3 labeling and tagging: L1 auto-tagged (scripts - team with judges help) L2 manual participants and captain verified action declarations (AI agent or a scripts as help - is an option) L3 Judge-validated on disputed events.

Forensic archive contains all of the above plus full raw PCAP, uncompressed logs, and raw video — approximately 3–4× the ML-ready volume. Dataset specifications subject to community validation and final format confirmation. Sizes are pre-event estimates; actual volumes may vary.
For Edition Z E R O - gathering of complete FA will depend on interest in it and participants agreement.

Behavioral Telemetry

  • Video from participant workspace / computer camera. Desktop view. Voice recordings.
  • Internal reports and inter-team communications (with consent).
  • Terminal sessions recorded as timestamped JSON objects (Asciinema).
  • Action log: timestamp, action type, tool, parameters, duration, effect estimation / proof.
  • Live reporting: simplified layer for judges; processed layer for commentary and summaries.
  • AI / bot monitoring is each team's responsibility; all activity is logged as part of the dataset.

ML-ready DS Processing Pipeline — Edge-to-Cold Model

  • Edge: selective incident and action logs (Sysmon, EDR, Firewall), packet metadata (NetFlow). Full PCAP on incident triggers only, reducing overall volume by approximately 90%.
  • Smart behavioral compression: resolution, frame rate and compression adapted per source and activity level.

  • Pre-upload: flagging, manual reports (if required), compression, secure delivery.
  • Post-process: backup and anonymity / exposition tier implementation, storage and access control.

In Edition ZERO it is possible that gathering and reporting will be partially self-scripted or manual. Software solutions will be provided from the outset where possible.

D · LICENSING MODEL
D Licensing Model & Pre-Sale Access COMMERCIAL · EDU · AI LICENSE · RESTRICTIONS
#licencing +

We understand the risks to commercial companies' reputation and operations. Therefore offer anonymity and data exposure control to maximize gains and avoid harm. All data used outside the event adheres to strict rules available for evaluation prior to participation.

License Types

  • Participant license — access to match telemetry participated in, free of charge. Data for AI usage terms & conditions agreement free of charge (team choice: complete or curated&compressed).
  • Observer license — tiered access to curated datasets per release policy. Requires NDA and observer agreement.
  • Educational license — EDU FREE: Slightly Simplified, Reports & Exploits DS - access from Month 10 after the benchmark. Requires NDA, observer agreement, license for AI.
  • Judges license — full dataset access of judged events, pool of free DS and fixed low fee price for not included DS.
  • AI training license — additional license required on top of any base license. Subject to contractual penalty for unauthorized use or distribution.

Restrictions

  • Selling, copying or redistributing datasets is prohibited without organizer authorization.
  • AI training requires an explicit AI license; violation subject to disclosure of model code or substantial contractual penalty.
  • Data may not be used in any harmful, discriminatory or misleading way.
  • Non-anonymised data is never sold and remains offline after delivery.
  • Participants are prohibited from using undeclared monitoring during the event.
  • Servers must be secured according to published guidelines.

AI Training & Model Development License


⚠ Additional license required · Contractual penalty applies

Any use of ECB datasets for AI model training, fine-tuning, RLHF, or derivative weight generation requires an explicit AI license in addition to the base dataset license. E · SECURITY & STORAGE
E Data Safety & Storage OFFLINE FIRST · ENCRYPTED · CONTROLLED EXPOSURE
#security +

We guarantee offline storage of all non-anonymised data. Non-anonymised data is not exposed, used externally, or sold and is stored only for legal and tournament management purposes. Video data for any purpose other than research and development is covered by an independent agreement and separate policies. This setting allows easy changes in privacy tiers or visualization themes.

Storage Architecture

  • Primary storage: offline, on separated HDD/SSD drives, physically secured.
  • Online aggregation (interim): Wasabi or Backblaze B2 until own dedicated infrastructure is operational.
  • Analytical server: VPS (e.g. Hetzner / OVH) for processing and labeling pipeline.

Guarantees

  • Offline storage guaranteed for all non-anonymised data.
  • Controlled exposure — participants define their own visibility at all times.
  • Inner rankings and private benchmarks — never public by default.
  • Simplified, non-identifiable data used publicly only.
  • All data used outside the event adheres to strict rules available prior for evaluation.

Organizer Obligations

The organizers will inform supporters, observers, partners and all interested parties of any limitations, issues, delays or problems at the earliest possible time. Progress and stage of preparations will be updated publicly. Great care will be taken to ensure flawless delivery.
