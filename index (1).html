Ensuring survival on individual and humankind level, but also planetary, endurance of alternatives, societies, and communities. We build alternatives. Our goal is: at least fine life for everyone - we believe that garden have to be built, it will not appear out of nowhere.

Enhanced.World is charity, science and technology related foundation, divided into three branches of projects and communities, governed by Capitules:

1. TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE: For Science, Research, Education, Development, Data Projects, Talents Support.
2. ETHICS & IMPACT: Promotion of Empathy; To offer and promote compassionate redress and unconditional support to these group: orphans, endangered children; painfully ill individuals; torture, sex crime and slavery victims);
3. DAOs, FORMATS & GOVERNANCE: Governance on Strategic and Monitoring level for the Foundation, q-DAO Social Economic System and broader formats. Promote and guard related entities regulations and general values of: Mutualism, Civic-mindedness, Fairness.
