Safe, responsible and publicly beneficial technology.

Enhanced.World is currently a collection of independent initiatives linked by a long-term vision: resilience, human well-being, safety and responsible technology. Immediate focus is on practical software systems, open collaboration, and securing a sustainable financial and organizational basis for a formal foundation.

Ensuring the ability to survive and thrive—for individuals, communities and humanity. Our aim is at least a decent life for everyone. A better future has to be built; it will not emerge on its own.

Current focus & practical work

Essential Crisis Software (ECS)

An open-source, modular crisis-resilience software initiative. Its proposed Crisis Resilience Network and related application pack are part of the same direction: connecting people and local groups through practical skills, knowledge, preparation and cooperation. Blueprints and initial documentation are available; co-development open source call is open Let's build together.

'Founder's Own: a-Tools ecosystem'

A family of practical automation and AI tools at different stages of development: 1-Click Apps (MyLocalAI, KnowledgeBackup, PublicationHQ), Communication Manager; Deep Companion; Extended HQ. Presets for: Free API / Fully Local / Hybrid (sensitive and public data differentiating). Individually released.

Additional long-term directions

FENI (responsible technology discussions), GEMSTONES (knowledge and skills preservation), personal safety and layered protection concepts, drones and areostats, memory and identity preservation, social support and charity, governance ideas and alternative economic models, including future qDAO-related exploration. These are aspirations rather than active programmes. Human-centred AI & interdisciplinary science. Longer-term interests include a psychologically aware AI companion, support for people experiencing hardship, robotics and autonomous systems, medicine, longevity and research partnerships. These are exploratory directions, not existing services.
